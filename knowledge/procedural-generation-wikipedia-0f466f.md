**Procedural Generation**
==========================

**Definition**
------------

Procedural generation is a method of creating data algorithmically, as opposed to manually, typically through a combination of human-generated content and algorithms coupled with computer-generated randomness and processing power.

**Applications**
--------------

*   **Computer Graphics**: Used to create textures, 3D models, and landscapes.
*   **Video Games**: Used to automatically create large amounts of content, such as levels, textures, and complete worlds, with minimal human contribution.
*   **Sound Generation**: Used in speech synthesis, music composition, and other applications.

**History**
------------

*   **Early Days**: Procedural generation was originally used in video games to generate levels, textures, and complete worlds with little human contribution.
*   **Tabletop Role-Playing Games**: Procedural generation was also used in tabletop RPGs, such as Dungeons & Dragons, to generate dungeons and terrain.
*   **Modern Developments**: Procedural generation has continued to evolve, with new methods and applications presented annually in conferences such as the IEEE Conference on Computational Intelligence and Games and the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Interactive Digital Entertainment.

**Key Concepts**
----------------

*   **Fractals**: Geometric patterns that can often be generated procedurally.
*   **Textures**: Often procedurally generated, used in computer graphics.
*   **Meshes**: Can be procedurally generated, used in computer graphics.
*   **Random Number Generators**: Used to generate pseudorandom numbers, often with predefined seed values.
*   **Map Seed**: A short number or text string used to procedurally create game worlds.

**Notable Examples**
-------------------

*   **The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall**: Features a mostly procedurally generated world.
*   **NetHack**: A roguelike game that heavily utilizes procedural generation to randomly produce dungeons.
*   **Just Cause**: Used procedural generation to create a large and varied group of detailed tropical islands.
*   **No Man's Sky**: A game that is all based upon procedurally generated elements.

**Challenges**
-------------

*   **Infinite Worlds**: Procedural systems can generate infinite numbers of worlds to explore, without sufficient human guidance and rules to guide these.
*   **Lack of Uniqueness**: Procedural generated content can be perceived as lacking the notion of uniqueness, compared to human-generated content.

**Conclusion**
--------------

Procedural generation is a powerful method of creating data algorithmically, with a wide range of applications in computer graphics, video games, and sound generation. While it has many advantages, it also presents challenges, such as the potential for infinite worlds and a lack of uniqueness.
