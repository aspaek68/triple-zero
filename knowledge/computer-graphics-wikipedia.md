# Computer Graphics
================

## Definition and Scope

Computer graphics (CG) is the generation of images and art with the aid of computers. It is a core technology in digital photography, film, video games, digital art, cell phone and computer displays, and many specialized applications.

## History

The term "computer graphics" was coined in 1960 by Verne Hudson and William Fetter of Boeing. The precursor sciences to the development of modern computer graphics were the advances in electrical engineering, electronics, and television during the first half of the twentieth century.

## Key Developments

* 1950s: Early projects like the Whirlwind and SAGE Projects introduced the CRT as a viable display and interaction interface and the light pen as an input device.
* 1958: William Higinbotham created the first interactive video game, "Tennis for Two," for an oscilloscope.
* 1959: Douglas T. Ross created a display scope image of a Disney cartoon character.
* 1957: Hewlett-Packard went public, establishing strong ties with Stanford University and contributing to the development of computer graphics.

## Types of Computer Graphics

* 2D computer graphics: Still widely used, these graphics are displayed on a two-dimensional screen.
* 3D computer graphics: Have become more common in recent years, these graphics are displayed on a three-dimensional screen.
* Animated graphics: These graphics are displayed in motion.

## Applications

## APIs and Tools

* Various APIs and tools have been developed to visualize data and create computer-generated imagery.
* Examples include Blender, which is a popular open-source 3D creation software.

* Representation and manipulation of image data by a computer
* Various technologies used to create and manipulate images
* Methods for digitally synthesizing and manipulating visual content

## Conclusion

Computer graphics is a vast and recently developed area of computer science. It has had a significant impact on many types of media and has revolutionized animation, movies, advertising, and video games.
