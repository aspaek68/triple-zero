**Artificial Intelligence**
========================

**Definition**
---------------

Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to the ability of computational systems to perform tasks typically associated with human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making.

**Key Concepts**
----------------

*   **Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)**: AI that can complete nearly any cognitive task at least as well as a human.
*   **Machine Learning**: A subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.
*   **Deep Learning**: A type of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns in data.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: AI that enables systems to understand, generate, and process human language.
*   **Robotics**: AI that enables systems to interact with and control physical devices.

**Applications**
----------------

*   **Web Search Engines**: AI-powered search engines that use machine learning to improve search results.
*   **Chatbots**: AI-powered conversational systems that use NLP to understand and respond to user input.
*   **Virtual Assistants**: AI-powered systems that use machine learning to perform tasks such as scheduling and reminders.
*   **Autonomous Vehicles**: AI-powered vehicles that use computer vision and machine learning to navigate and make decisions.
*   **Content Generation**: AI-powered systems that use machine learning to generate text, images, audio, and videos.

**Techniques**
--------------

*   **State Space Search**: A method used to solve problems by searching through a state space.
*   **Mathematical Optimization**: A method used to optimize functions and solve problems.
*   **Formal Logic**: A method used to reason and solve problems using formal rules and axioms.
*   **Artificial Neural Networks**: A type of machine learning that uses neural networks to learn complex patterns in data.

**History**
----------

*   **1956**: Artificial intelligence was founded as an academic discipline.
*   **1980s-1990s**: Researchers developed algorithms for dealing with uncertain or incomplete information.
*   **2000s**: Machine learning and deep learning became popular techniques for AI.
*   **2012**: Graphics processing units (GPUs) were used to accelerate neural networks, leading to a surge in AI research and development.

**Controversies**
----------------

*   **AI Safety**: Concerns about the potential risks and unintended consequences of AI systems.
*   **Ethical Concerns**: Concerns about the use of AI in areas such as surveillance, facial recognition, and content moderation.
*   **Existential Risk**: Concerns about the potential risks of creating advanced AI systems that could pose an existential threat to humanity.

**Glossary**
------------

*   **AGI**: Artificial General Intelligence.
*   **AI**: Artificial Intelligence.
*   **Machine Learning**: A subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.
*   **Deep Learning**: A type of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns in data.
